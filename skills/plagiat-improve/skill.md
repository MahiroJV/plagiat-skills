# plagiat-improve

A specialized rewriting and humanization workflow designed to transform AI-generated, formulaic, or flagged text into natural, original, and compelling prose.

## Summary

This skill rewrites content to eliminate synthetic structures, monotonous sentence cadences, and unoriginal phrasing. It produces an authentic, natural voice while strictly preserving the original meaning, arguments, and factual integrity.

## When to Use

- The user wants to rewrite text that was flagged for plagiarism or AI generation.
- The user asks to humanize text, make it sound more natural, or remove AI clichés.
- The user requests a rephrased, polished, or authentic version of a draft, essay, or article.
- The user asks to bypass rigid formulaic phrasing while retaining core ideas and data.

## Workflow Steps

### 1. Identify Synthetic and Problematic Patterns

Analyze the input text to locate the specific elements that need transformation:

- Repetitive sentence rhythms: Find clusters of sentences that share nearly identical lengths and grammatical structures.
- Hallmark AI clichés and filler: Identify overused markers such as "in conclusion", "it is crucial to note", "delve into", "a testament to", "rich tapestry", and passive hedging.
- Verbatim or formulaic phrasing: Spot rigid, textbook-style descriptions that trigger similarity checkers.

### 2. Deconstruct and Extract Core Arguments

Before rewriting, extract the essential substance:

- Isolate key facts, figures, technical concepts, and logical points.
- Identify the author's primary intent, stance, and target audience.
- Ensure no factual claims, data points, or specialized terms are altered or lost during rewriting.

### 3. Rewrite with Authentic Human Cadence

Reconstruct the text using dynamic, engaging, and varied prose:

- Vary sentence length and complexity (burstiness): Alternate short, punchy statements with longer, multi-clause thoughts.
- Use direct, active voice: Replace passive corporate jargon with clear, everyday verbs and vivid explanations.
- Replace formulaic transitions: Avoid mechanical connectors like "Furthermore", "Moreover", and "In summary". Connect paragraphs through logical flow and thematic progression.
- Adapt tone to context: Match the requested register (academic, professional, conversational, or creative) without defaulting to generic neutrality.

### 4. Provide the Deliverable

Present the improved output clearly:

- Improved Text: Deliver the complete, polished version ready to use.
- Key Adjustments: Briefly highlight major changes made (e.g., varied cadence, removed repetitive transitions, rephrased verbatim expressions).

## Gotchas

- Do not alter factual meaning: Never invent facts or change the argument's premise to achieve originality.
- Do not over-embellish: Avoid swapping simple words for overly obscure vocabulary; natural human prose is clear, not unnecessarily convoluted.
- Preserve technical definitions: Domain-specific terms, laws, and official names must remain accurate.
